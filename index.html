    How it works

    Ratings

    Plays since 2016 feed weighted ridge regressions for dropback EPA, rush EPA, dropback and rush success rate, neutral-situation pass rate and plays per game, each split into offense, defense and home effects. Recent weeks count most (a play's weight halves about every 8 weeks) and last season's plays get a further 16% discount, so this season is about 45% of the picture by Week 5 and 85% by Week 18. Starting QBs get their own term that follows them across teams.

    Injuries, wind, travel

    Injury clusters count starters ruled Out or Doubtful, weighted by their snap share over the last four games, so a starter who just went down counts fully and a long-term absence is already in the ratings. Wind above 10 mph at outdoor games lowers totals. West-coast teams in early Eastern kickoffs have covered more often than the market expects.

    Every-game picks

    The model picks a side against the consensus spread in every game. Its confidence is the share of picks like it that covered in the walk-forward backtest, shrunk toward 50%. High means that rate clears break-even at −110 with room; the rest are close to coin flips. Picks lock with the bets and are graded at the locked line.

    Parlay builder

    Every run rebuilds 2- and 3-leg parlays from the highest-confidence legs of any type (an estimated win rate of 53%+: High every-game spread picks today; totals and proven bets join automatically once their record clears that bar) and 2- and 3-team teasers from underdogs at +1.5/+2.5, only when the legs’ estimated win rates make the parlay worth more than it costs. A spread + big-total parlay is tracked on paper to test whether the model’s biggest total gaps belong in parlays. The card updates every run and locks once, with the final picks Saturday night; each type keeps its own record. Types that lose out of sample stay on paper.

    Tiers

    Best Bet a model edge that an independent edge agrees with. Play spread gap 1.5–3 or total gap 4–8, the ranges that have paid. Angle a standalone edge (wind, injury cluster, travel) the model doesn't contradict. Verify big gaps, which usually mean news the model can't see.

    Self-learning

    Every run archives lines, Polymarket prices, projections, starters, forecasts and injury reads. Weekly, the lab re-tests model settings walk-forward and promotes one only if it beats the champion by a statistically significant margin. A discovery engine searches thousands of candidate rules, keeps the few that replicate on later seasons, and tracks them on paper. A live monitor promotes or retires rules from their real results.

    Timing and grading

    Picks stay live all week: every run (Sunday night, Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday morning) re-projects every game with the latest lines, injury reports, quarterback news and forecasts, so bets can appear, change or drop out. Each game locks once, at the last run before it: Saturday at 9:50 PM PT, right after the evening line capture, for Sunday and Monday games; the morning of the game for Thursday, Friday and Saturday games. If the Saturday-night lines don't arrive, Sunday's 5:50 AM run locks instead. Bets are graded at the locked line, along with closing-line value (how far the market moved by kickoff), and every run's picks are archived.

    Data: nflverse play-by-play, schedules, injury reports and snap counts; sportsbook consensus lines via nflverse; Polymarket game markets; forecasts and injury news from public reports. Projections are estimates, not advice. Bet within limits you set in advance.
