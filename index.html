    How it works

    Ratings

    Plays since 2016 feed weighted ridge regressions for dropback EPA, rush EPA, dropback and rush success rate, neutral-situation pass rate and plays per game, each split into offense, defense and home effects. Recent weeks count most (a play's weight halves about every 8 weeks) and last season's plays get a further 16% discount, so this season is about 45% of the picture by Week 5 and 85% by Week 18. Starting QBs get their own term that follows them across teams.

    Injuries, wind, travel

    Injury clusters count starters ruled Out or Doubtful, weighted by their snap share over the last four games, so a starter who just went down counts fully and a long-term absence is already in the ratings. Wind above 10 mph at outdoor games lowers totals. West-coast teams in early Eastern kickoffs have covered more often than the market expects.

    Every-game picks (retired Oct 11, 2026)

    Through Week 5 of 2026 the model picked a side against the consensus spread in every game. They were retired because picks this close to a good line are coin flips (within 1.5 points: 376–383 since 2018); from Week 6 each game card shows that game's actual bets (or "none yet") instead. How they worked: Its confidence is the share of picks like it that covered in the walk-forward backtest, shrunk toward 50%. High means that rate clears break-even at −110 with room; the rest are close to coin flips. Picks lock with the bets and are graded at the locked line. They're picks, not bets: since Oct 10 spread bets are on paper (High picks went 131–130 in 2023–2025), and they no longer feed paid parlays.

    Long shots and parlays

    Long shots (since Oct 12): each betting window gets the 3-, 4- and 5-leg parlays of its best real bets, one leg per game, ranked by estimated win rate, locked with the first Sunday game-day lock (until Oct 16, 2026 also with the early card on Sunday night), 1% of the bankroll each; built to hit rarely and pay big. Teasers and short parlays: Every run rebuilds 2- and 3-leg parlays from the highest-confidence legs of any type (an estimated win rate of 53%+: active rules and totals whose record clears that bar; High every-game spread picks were legs until they were retired Oct 11) and 2- and 3-team teasers from underdogs at +1.5/+2.5, only when the legs’ estimated win rates make the parlay worth more than it costs. A spread + big-total parlay is tracked on paper to test whether the model’s biggest total gaps belong in parlays. The card updates every run and locks once, with the first Sunday game-day lock (it was Saturday night until Oct 16, 2026); each type keeps its own record. Types that lose out of sample stay on paper.

    Tiers

    Best Bet a model edge that an independent edge agrees with. Play total gap 4–8 where injuries move the total 1+ point toward the bet (100–65 since 2018; gaps from team ratings alone went 110–111, so they're on paper). Spread gaps of 1.5–3 went 121–130 in 2023–2025, so since Oct 10 they're graded on paper until live results earn them back (they still become Best Bets when an independent edge agrees). Angle a standalone edge (wind, injury cluster, travel) the model doesn't contradict. Verify big gaps, which usually mean news the model can't see.

    Self-learning

    Every run archives lines, Polymarket prices, projections, starters, forecasts and injury reads. Weekly, the lab re-tests model settings walk-forward and promotes one only if it beats the champion by a statistically significant margin. A discovery engine searches thousands of candidate rules, keeps the few that replicate on later seasons, and tracks them on paper. A live monitor promotes or retires rules from their real results.

    Timing and grading

    Game-day locks (since Oct 16, 2026). Nothing is locked days ahead. Every run all week (Sunday night, Monday to Wednesday mornings, every weeknight, Thursday and Friday mornings, Saturday morning and night) re-projects every game with the latest lines, injury reports, quarterback news, referees and forecasts, so live bets can appear, change or drop out. Each game locks once, on game day, at the last run that finishes before kickoff, right after the inactives capture: Sunday 9:10 AM PT for the 10 AM games, 12:10 PM for the late games, 4:10 PM for Sunday night; 4:10 PM for Monday and Thursday night (12:10 PM for an early Monday game); Saturday 9:50 PM for a Sunday-morning international game. That leaves you about half an hour to place a bet, so watch for the message. Only locked bets count and get stakes. The early-week card (the model against the line right after it opens) is still snapshotted Sunday night and graded on paper, so the lab keeps measuring whether betting early would have paid. If a lock run gets no fresh sportsbook lines it holds, and that game isn’t tracked that week. Bets are graded at the locked line, along with closing-line value: the bet's number against the books' consensus in the last capture before kickoff (within 3 hours of it; otherwise the nflverse closing line), plus how far the consensus itself moved after the lock. A bet that only makes sense if a questionable quarterback plays is held on paper until he is confirmed, because a sportsbook grades it whoever starts. Every run's picks are archived.

    Data: nflverse play-by-play, schedules, injury reports and snap counts; sportsbook consensus lines via nflverse; Polymarket game markets; forecasts and injury news from public reports. Projections are estimates, not advice. Bet within limits you set in advance.
